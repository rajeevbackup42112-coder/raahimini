# Raahi 2.0 — Pre-Launch Legal Review Checklist

**Purpose:** Counsel-review handoff for the launch Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Driver Terms. This is an operational checklist, not legal advice.

## Product model counsel should review

Raahi coordinates local mobility through two models: route-based Shared Ride and Driver-quoted Outstation travel. At launch, transportation payment is intended to be made directly by Passenger to Driver, Raahi charges no platform commission, and Raahi does not itself drive or own the vehicle used for an independent Driver trip.

The legal review should not assume that this functional description determines Raahi's statutory classification. Counsel should separately determine whether central/State motor-vehicle or aggregator law applies to any launch activity.

## Decisions required before public launch

- Insert the legal operator/proprietor/entity name, principal/registered address, grievance officer, grievance email/phone and final jurisdiction into the public legal pages.
- Confirm the correct contracting party and whether any trade-name/proprietorship/company disclosure is required around the Raahi brand.
- Determine whether the Motor Vehicle Aggregator Guidelines, 2025 and/or State aggregator rules/licensing apply to Raahi's Shared Ride or Outstation model in each launch State.
- If aggregator rules apply, map operational gaps beyond Terms: licence, Driver welfare/insurance, document authentication, complaint inquiry, control-room/call-centre, safety and other prescribed obligations.
- Review Driver document/permit/insurance/fitness requirements for each vehicle/service category actually allowed on Raahi.
- Confirm treatment of direct Passenger-to-Driver fares, tax/GST obligations, invoicing and any future Raahi commission or platform fee.
- Confirm cancellation/no-show/refund wording and whether a formal cancellation policy must be published before launch.
- Confirm consumer-grievance disclosures and Consumer Protection / E-Commerce Rules applicability.
- Review age/minor-travel rules and whether Raahi should prohibit unaccompanied minors entirely at launch.
- Confirm governing-law/jurisdiction wording without restricting mandatory consumer forums.

## Privacy / DPDP review

- Review the Privacy Policy against the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 and their staged commencement timeline.
- Confirm the final Data Fiduciary identity/contact disclosures and grievance process.
- Approve retention periods for trip records, GPS, audit/support history and Driver verification material.
- Confirm the lawful basis/notice/consent model for authentication, phone verification, GPS, safety investigations, Driver verification and aggregate mobility analytics.
- Confirm incident/breach response obligations and processor/service-provider contract requirements.
- Review any cross-border processing by Supabase, Netlify, Google or future communications providers.

## Product clauses counsel should specifically challenge

- Raahi's coordination-platform description versus any non-waivable aggregator/carrier/operator obligations.
- Driver verification disclaimer: verification is a trust control, not a guarantee.
- Direct payment wording and the absence of a Raahi-controlled refund flow at launch.
- Balanced limitation-of-liability language; no waiver of mandatory consumer/statutory rights.
- Driver independent-status wording; applicable law expressly prevails over contractual labels.
- Local Offers/sponsored promotion disclaimer and the launch promise not to sell personal data or build unrelated trip-history ad profiles.
- Versioned electronic acceptance and retention of historic acceptance timestamps.

## Current official references reviewed during drafting

- Ministry of Road Transport & Highways — Motor Vehicle Aggregator Guidelines, 2025.
- Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology — Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 and enforcement-timeline notifications.
- Department of Consumer Affairs — Consumer Protection Act / Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules materials.
